# Purview Gurukul

Static training app for Purview Healthcare's RCM teams, published with GitHub Pages. No backend: progress, quiz results and certificates live in the viewer's browser and print from the page. The Dashboard tab (same OTP lock) shows, for every project and specialty track, whether its training material is up to date with the reason when it is not, who manages and leads it and its scope of work, and how many certificates have been issued by step and by project. Certification ends with a final readiness test: one assessment across the RCM Basics course and AR and denial management (no client process content), scored by area with a ready / not yet verdict.

## Files

- `index.html` – the whole app (styles, markup, engine).
- `basics.js` – the RCM Basics course (modules, topics, quiz bank, discussion prompts, plain-language aliases).
- `scenarios.js`, `calls.js` – generated scenario cases and payer-call role play.
- `sources.js` – official source links per topic and the quarterly review schedule.
- `sops.js` – general Purview SOP placeholders.
- `sops-abw.js` – the ABW Medical client playbook and its assessment questions, from the AR Notes Reference Guide, the consolidated Client Operations Manual and the ABW athenaOne SOPs (navigation, Hold and MGR Hold, kick codes, Zero Pay, Unpostables, patient AR and reports; Missing Slips is left out while it is not in ABW's scope), with ABW's answers to Purview's questions; names, contacts, account numbers and screenshots deliberately left out.
- `sops-hagion.js` – the Hagion (Joppa Trading and Medical Supplies) client playbook and its assessment questions, from the Process Flow SOP, the NikoHealth Navigation SOP, the Update Tracker and the client meeting minutes (staff names, phone numbers, shared-drive links and screenshots with patient details left out). It adds itself to the client list, so it also appears as a process in Certification.
- `sops-titan.js` – the Titan (Northwestern Medicine ARR) client playbook and its assessment questions, from Titan's collector manual and RevX guide, the NWM ARR training and reference guide, the Known Billing Issues list and the Epic navigation SOP, with Titan's answers on the points where those documents disagreed (phone and fax numbers, addresses, hospital tax IDs and NPIs, staff names, internal links and patient details left out). It also adds itself to the client list and appears as a process in Certification.
- `sops-vec.js` – the VEC (Village Eye Care) client playbook and its assessment questions, from the VEC AR Work Flow SOP and the PCC and EV SOP (staff names, the contact list with its email addresses, and screenshots left out). It also adds itself to the client list and appears as a process in Certification.
- Client updates: each playbook in `sops-*.js` can carry `updates:[{date:"YYYY-MM-DD",by,title,summary,lessons:[ids]}]`, newest anywhere in the list, and each topic an update touches carries `upd:"YYYY-MM-DD"`. The playbook then shows a "What changed" panel (date, who shared it, summary, links to the topics), an "Updated" badge on those topics for 30 days, and a dated banner inside each one.
- `specialties.js` – the registry for the Specialties tab (`SPEC_TRACKS`, `SPEC_QUIZ`); each specialty file adds itself to it.
- `spec-vision.js` – the Vision care specialty track and its assessment questions: the eye visit (exam, refraction, contact lenses, CL fitting), the codes on a vision claim (CPT eye codes, S0620/S0621, contact lens fitting codes, HCPCS V codes, ICD-10), vision plans versus medical plans and COB, and the common vision denials, plus a fifth module from the Vision claim submission training: submission methods and the COB form, the prescription-to-diagnosis rule and the diagnosis list by category, the test and imaging codes with their modifiers, and in-office procedures with cataract co-management. Built from Purview's Vision care RCM guide for freshers, with the code definitions checked against CPT, HCPCS and Medicare.gov; it links to the VEC playbook and the vision denials in the library, and appears as a specialty in Certification.
- `spec-dental.js` – the Dental care specialty track and its assessment questions: the mouth map (types of teeth, quadrants, Universal tooth numbers, surfaces), the common procedures and X-rays, the dental specialties (endodontics, periodontics, oral surgery, orthodontics, prosthodontics, pediatric dentistry) and what each bills, CDT codes and ICD-10 on dental claims, the ADA Dental Claim Form, attachments and predeterminations, how dental plans pay (classes, annual maximum, deductibles, waiting periods, frequency limits and other clauses, with the missing tooth clause and downgrades (the alternate benefit) as topics of their own), COB and dental-to-medical cross-coding, the common dental denials, the patient's share and compliance. Built from Purview's Dental illustrations, Dental Specialities and Dental Plans guides (their pictures are not reproduced), checked against the ADA claim form instructions, CDT, ICD-10-CM and Medicare.gov; it appears as a second specialty in Certification.
- `spec-bh.js` – the Behavioral health, IOP and ABA specialty track (a sixth module covers outpatient psychiatry billing, behavioral health integration and collaborative care, digital mental health treatment, incident-to, and ABA therapy with its 97151–97158 codes) and its assessment questions: conditions, ASAM levels of care, therapies, clinician credentials and 42 CFR Part 2; F-code diagnoses and the dual-diagnosis order; psychotherapy, SUD and IOP codes, EMDR, POS and modifiers; IOP per diem billing (H0015, S9480, revenue codes 0905 and 0906 with the pairing set per payer, minimum hours, same-day exclusions), the UB-04 field by field and the pre-submission checklist; Medicare IOP for RHCs and FQHCs (CR 13264); prior authorization, telehealth, denials and AR cleanup; and the Kentucky Medicaid IOP rules as a state example (five MCOs, deadlines, one claim per rolling seven-day week). Built from the UB-04 & IOP training module and a Kentucky behavioral health billing operations guide v2.0 (reference material only; the provider it was written for is not a client and is not named), with the module's code errors corrected; no provider names, addresses or contacts are reproduced.
- `sw.js` – offline cache; bump `VER` on every release.
- `tools/gurukul-email.gs` – the Google Apps Script that does Gurukul's email and holds the Dashboard's data: it sends feedback (with up to three screenshots) straight to the maintainers, it sends and checks the one-time password that opens the Certification and Dashboard pages for approved email IDs, and it stores, in its Script Properties, each project's training-material status (yes, no or other, with the reason), manager, team lead and scope of work, plus the count of certificates issued by step and by project (a fingerprint of each certified email ID, never the name). Dashboard calls carry a signed access token issued at OTP time. After updating the script, deploy it as a new version and verify your email ID in Certification once more. The approved list is kept in the script's Script Properties (`CERT_EMAILS`), not in this public code. Setup steps are at the top of the file; put its web app URL in `SCRIPT_URL` in `index.html`. Until then, Feedback opens the user's own mail app and Certification stays locked.
